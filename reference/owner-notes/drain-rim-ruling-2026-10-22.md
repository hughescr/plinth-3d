Craig 2026-10-22 (Sphere, drain at the lowest point; keep-out 2.85 mm = bore 1.25 + max arm 1.0 + 0.6 left layers 100–106 unsupported): "the clearance rule says 'don't place contacts right by the drain hole so that resin can flow properly' but in the sphere case, we can remain clear of blocking resin flow while still supporting the lowest layers with thin support that don't enter the cylinder below the hole - but they can come right up to the edge and not be blocking." And: "hair tips can lean in and just touch the edge of the hole without blocking drainage."

**Why:** the keep-out exists only to keep the drain mouth open; a fixed 2.85 mm radius left a domed bottom's first 7 layers floating.
**How to apply:** support solids must not enter the bore cylinder projected down from the hole; outside it they may stand right at the edge, sized per arm (not the max-arm radius). Hair tips may lean in to hold the first rim layer. Warn whenever a rim layer is left unseated. Diagnosis: plinth-evidence/perf-2026-10-14/dr/REPORT.md.
