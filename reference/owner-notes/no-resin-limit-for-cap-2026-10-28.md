Asked how much extra support resin per model is acceptable to get every contact under 0.8 mm (options: ≤10%, ≤25%, none), Craig chose **"No limit"**.

**Why:** the 0.8 mm cap is a hard limit in the file. Resin is a cost to report, not a gate.
**How to apply:**
- Never reject or block a cap-meeting change because it uses more resin.
- Report the resin of the written support union against a matched cap-off file, with the raft shown separately.
- Waste is still a defect: extra geometry that buys no improvement should still lose to the incumbent. "No limit" is not permission to bloat.
