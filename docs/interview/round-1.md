# Owner interview — round 1 (2026-10-29)
Answers in Craig's words, lightly condensed. Interpretation notes marked "Note:".

1. **Print history.** Nothing from plinth was ever printed.
2. **Records.** No H2 plate file or pieces; Craig did not know what "H2" was. Note: H2 = the "Home Support Calibration P1" coupon plate (rows of posts with heads, 76 IDs); photos 05–09 show it with its printed protocol sheets behind. Photos 01–04 and 10–11 are plate 6 (labelled specimens: S0/S1/M3/PB/L7/L1, M4/M1/L3/S7/M2, KL, 3WX/3WY, 4TY, Z1/Z2 …). All saved in `reference/experiments/plate-photos/owner-originals-2026-10-29/`. No ChituBox files for those plates exist. `cylinder-cube-sphere.ctp` WAS written by ChituBox (its proprietary format, exported in case it was worth reverse-engineering; no plan to use it).
3. **Current practice with ChituBox.** A macro: auto-orient, then "magic support" with "light support" (medium and smaller minis) or "better support" (large and huge) — the choice is subjective. Then "overhang detection" and hand-added ultralight supports on the few spots that look risky. Slice, look at the islands the slicer reports (usually not substantial), print. Success roughly 75–90%; failures usually supports too flimsy to hold the model → adjust and re-print. He cannot tell in advance whether a print will succeed.
4. **Done for v1.** (b): editing in ChituBox is acceptable IF he knows what kinds of edit might be needed and those edits are easy. Unedited is preferred. Easy edit = adding supports where ChituBox flags an overhang. Hard edit = moving supports.
5. **Target order.** Round bases → the "slightly fancier" Morel base → Bat Cat Owl (small, fairly simple) → Deinonychus. Giant Bat matters because it triggers hollowing. "Advanced mode": the treant (Awakened Tree) — complex hollowing, difficult crown features; supporting it in ChituBox is extremely difficult, and it is why he started the project.

Note for the PRD: the measurable gap vs today is (a) predictability ("can't tell ahead of time") and (b) the 10–25% failure rate from supports too weak, and (c) the treant being impractical by hand. Output should say where hand additions may be needed.
