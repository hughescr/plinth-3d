## FINDINGS — from the source at `d711586` / G3 tip, not from the parent design's assumptions

### F-G6-1. What one committed merge actually invalidates: contact indices and owner ids shift globally, but as an order-preserving remap; every survivor's eligible-row set is bit-invariant

Read from `append_variant_load_inputs` (`specialized.rs:6392`) and `consolidate`
(`specialized.rs:2905–2920`):

- **Owner ids and contact ids are positional.** `append_variant_load_inputs` assigns
  `contact_id = position` (dense `0..n`, enforced by `SpecializedLoadBaseline::build`,
  `perception.rs:3720`) and a specialized owner
  `first_specialized_owner + contact_id` (`specialized.rs:6439`). So after a commit removes
  contacts `l < r` and `next.sort_by(compare_contacts)` inserts the merged seat at its sorted
  position `p` (NOT last — the parent design's D2b warning stands), **every contact index and
  every specialized owner id past the perturbation points changes value.** The perturbation is
  global in *value*, order-preserving in *structure*: `sort_by` is stable, survivors were
  already sorted, so survivor relative order is preserved exactly.
- **Consequence for the row-major eligibility:** every row's `eligible_owners` list (the
  ~7.93 M `LoadOwner::Specialized(owner)` entries) changes *values* each round even though the
  contact↔face structure it encodes is unchanged. Retaining the row-major structure means
  remapping ~8 M owner ids per round. **Do not retain it — it is not needed** (F-G6-3).
- **Consequence for the contact-major transpose:** contact `c`'s bucket (`rows_of(c)`) depends
  only on which faces `c` serves, which depends only on `c`'s own routes and demands
  (`visit_served_owner_faces`, `specialized.rs:6320`; `parts_serves_owner`, `:6226`). A commit
  does not touch any survivor's routes or demands (`commit_merge`, `:6884`, builds a new
  `WorkingContact` and leaves survivors alone). **Survivor buckets are content-invariant; only
  their position in the CSR shifts.** The merged seat's bucket is the ascending dedup union of
  `l`'s and `r`'s buckets: `commit_merge` gives the merged contact the dedup-union of routes and
  demands, so `parts_serves_owner` over the merged contact equals the OR over the parts, its
  served-face set is the union of face sets, and its eligible rows are the linear merge of the
  two parents' ascending buckets — exactly the `elig(l) ∪ elig(r)` shape
  `GateLoadState::merged_rows` (`specialized.rs:6522`) already computes. This holds **only when
  the merged seat resolves specialized**; `generic_owner_for_parts` returning `Some` breaks it,
  which is why `merged_seat_is_specialized` (`:6578`) becomes a retention guard (D-G6-3, guard
  3). Production merge batches always resolve specialized (both members carry demands), so the
  guard is expected never to fire — it exists for the silent-wrongness case.
- **Row-side invariance is already measured and argued:** row set constant at 117,741 across
  rounds (G4 invariant; FU-8d F3's ∪-argument; parent design F-5), row order determined by
  face id with base-order tie-break (`extend_load_eligibility`, `perception.rs:3364`), row
  areas/centroids read from base or `surface` — all merge-invariant. **Old row indices are new
  row indices.** Pairs shrink by ~100–330/round (the parents' face-set overlap); contacts by
  exactly 1.

**Answer to the design question's part 1, plainly:** the delta is NOT local in index space — a
merge renumbers every contact and owner id past the insertion point, and every CSR offset. But
it IS local in *content*: one bucket is removed twice and inserted once (as a union), everything
else is a positional remap. A retention design that stores buckets by contact position and
remaps positions is O(affected) in content and O(contacts) in bookkeeping; nothing forces
O(cells) work.

### F-G6-2. Where the 813 ms went: G1's deliberate self-containment is the thing G6 retires

`LoadTransposeCounts::count` + `::finish` (`perception.rs:2538`, `:2603`) each walk all
`Σ|eligible_owners|` ≈ 7.93 M `(row, owner)` entries with a `partition_point` over the
owner-sorted `order` array per entry — **two full binary-search passes per round** (count, then
fill), measured at 405 + 408 = **813 ms probe-on** (~625 ms probe-off share of the 772–829 ms
`build_gate_load_state`). G1 chose this self-contained build *deliberately* ("do not yet reuse
`pair_starts`/`face_row`, which would couple this to the gate") to keep the phase narrow. That
was the right phase-1 call and it is exactly what G6 now removes: the transpose is rebuilt from
scratch every round, walking ~158 M logical cells' worth of derivation, to reflect a delta of
one committed merge.

### F-G6-3. The retained path does not need the row-major eligibility at all — `grouped_from_pairs` (134 ms) and `extend_load_eligibility` (17 ms) fall out for free

`scan_transposed_load` (`perception.rs:2671`) reads only: the transpose, `contacts[].point_mm`,
and `load_eligibility[].centroid_mm`. The post-scan fill (`finalize_load_rows:2940`) reads only
`face_id`, `area_mm2` per row and the winner. `SpecializedLoadBaseline::build`'s post-pass reads
the retention witnesses and `FinalizedContactLoads::from_physical_contacts` (4.5 ms). So a
retained round needs: **the transpose (retained), per-row `face_id`/`area_mm2`/`centroid_mm`
(retained, merge-invariant), and the fresh `PhysicalContact` list (rebuilt, 12 ms).** The
extended `Vec<LoadEligibility>` — whose construction is `GroupedOwnerFaces::from_pairs`
(134 ms) + `extend_load_eligibility` (17 ms) — is consumed only by the transpose build and the
count-based charging. Retain the transpose and both **fall out of retained rounds naturally**,
as the design question hoped. They still run on full-build rounds (first round, guard-miss
rounds).

### F-G6-4. Contacts mutate outside the commit at two known sites — the guard must be equality, not trust

`attach_exact_mixed_contacts` (`specialized.rs:2817`) mutates contacts **before** the initial
proposal-free evaluate (harmless — retention doesn't exist yet), and
`coalesce_exact_regular_seats` (`:2929`) mutates them **after** the loop, before the terminal
proposal-free audit. The terminal audit therefore MUST NOT consume retained state updated only
by `record_commit`. The guard that handles this — and every future mutation site nobody
remembers to wire — is per-round **equality of the actual fresh `PhysicalContact` list against
the retained witness list** (D-G6-3, guard 2), not a round counter that trusts callers.

### F-G6-5. A free cardinality cross-check already runs every round

`build_gate_load_state` (`specialized.rs:4284–4296`) computes `contact_rows[c]` — each
contact's eligible-row count — from the **fresh** pair list every round (it feeds
`proposal_load_bound` and stays regardless). Comparing `contact_rows[c] ==
retained_bucket_len(c)` for every contact is an O(contacts) check of retained bucket
cardinality against freshly-derived truth, at zero added derivation cost. It cannot catch a
permuted bucket, but combined with the ascending `debug_assert`s and the mutation-proven test
harness it closes the cheap half of the content-drift surface at runtime.

### F-G6-6. Retention removes the build, not the scan — and the post-G6 leader is the baseline coverage sweep

`scan_transposed_load` (47 ms probe-on, ~36 ms probe-off) is per-round O(cells) and stays; so
do `append_variant_load_inputs` (12 ms), the `contact_rows`/`face_row` walks, the loads fold
(4.7 ms) and `baseline_sweep` (~106–128 ms). After G6 the round's leader is the baseline
coverage sweep at ~60 % of the round — the parent design's D2b table already specified its
retention (source-slot invariance, 0.42 % perturbed-key re-sweep). That is **G6e, contingent**
(D-G6-6): not scheduled unless the re-measure says the margin needs it.

## D-G6-1 — THE RETAINED STRUCTURE AND ITS OWNERSHIP

```rust
/// Lives in perception.rs beside LoadEligibilityTranspose. Crate-private.
pub(crate) struct RetainedLoadTranspose {
    /// Bucket payloads, append-only arena. A committed merge appends the
    /// merged bucket; parents' payloads become dead until compaction.
    arena: Vec<u32>,
    /// Per contact, in current contact order: (offset, len) into `arena`.
    buckets: Vec<(u32, u32)>,
    /// Live cells = Σ len. Maintained by ± deltas; the charged scan size.
    cells: usize,
    /// Row invariants, retained from the full build, never touched by updates:
    row_face_id: Vec<u32>,      // FinalizedFaceLoad::face_id per row
    row_area_mm2: Vec<f64>,
    row_centroid_mm: Vec<[f64; 3]>,
    /// The contact list this state is synchronized to: (owner, point_mm) per
    /// contact, in order. contact_id is positional so it is not stored.
    contact_witness: Vec<(LoadOwner, [f64; 3])>,
    /// The mesh fingerprint the full build ran under (G2's proof digest).
    mesh_fingerprint: u64,
}
```

**Ownership and lifecycle.** `consolidate` (`specialized.rs:2796`) owns one
`Option<RetainedLoadTranspose>` on its stack, created `None`, threaded as
`&mut Option<RetainedLoadTranspose>` through `evaluate_gate_batch` into
`build_gate_load_state`, and **dropped when `consolidate` returns** — retention never escapes
the tournament. This is the smallest ownership change that works: `evaluate_gate_batch` keeps
its signature shape (one new `&mut` parameter beside the G3 `acceptance` parameter), prep stays
conceptually per-round, and the state's lifetime is exactly the stage the invariants hold for.
The accepted plan's own load finalize after `consolidate`, `derive_child`, and every non-gate
caller of `finalize_specialized_load_partition` pass nothing and are untouched.

**Lifecycle for models that finish in few rounds:** the initial proposal-free evaluate performs
the full build (it must anyway) and populates retention; round 1's evaluate finds the witness
equal (nothing mutated contacts between them — F-G6-4) and reuses it, so even the no-merge case
saves one full build. A model with zero gate batches never constructs the struct. Peak memory
is unchanged in kind: the transpose was allocated every round already; retention extends its
lifetime across rounds without stacking (one instance, replaced in place).

**In `build_gate_load_state`, the branch is:**

1. Build `physical`/`owner_faces`/`pair_starts` fresh (12 ms — unchanged; still feeds
   `merged_rows` and `proposal_load_bound`), then `face_row`/`row_face`/`contact_rows` — but
   note `face_row`/`row_face` derive from the partition, so on the retained path they derive
   from `row_face_id` (identical by row-set invariance).
2. **Guard chain** (D-G6-3). All pass → retained path; any miss → full build (today's exact
   code path, `SpecializedLoadBaseline::build` → `finalize_specialized_load_partition`)
   followed by `RetainedLoadTranspose` (re)population from the build's own transpose and
   partition. Guard misses are counted (census counter `retained_load_rebuilds`), never silent.
3. **Retained path:** run `scan_transposed_load` over the retained buckets (identical loop
   body, contacts ascending, buckets ascending — identical visit sequence to a fresh build, so
   bit-identity of `nearest` and all witness slots is by the same construction G1 proved);
   run the `finalize_load_rows` tail verbatim (finite/duplicate contact checks, row fill from
   retained `row_face_id`/`row_area_mm2`, contact FNV hash — all O(rows + contacts), measured
   at ≤1 ms each); run the `SpecializedLoadBaseline` post-pass (winner/won CSR, 4.7 ms) and
   `FinalizedContactLoads::from_physical_contacts` (4.5 ms) **fresh — carried areas are
   re-summed in the fold's ascending row order every round, never adjusted incrementally**
   (f64 addition is not associative; F3's own rule, carried over verbatim).

The scan-with-indirection loop is `for contact in 0..n { for &row in
&arena[off..off+len] }` — one added indirection per contact, not per cell; visit order is
unchanged, so the bit-identity argument is G1's argument unmodified.

## D-G6-2 — THE INCREMENTAL UPDATE

`RetainedLoadTranspose::record_commit(contacts_after_sort, left, right)` is called by
`consolidate` immediately after `*contacts = next` (`specialized.rs:2920`), before the next
round's gather. It:

1. **Derives the insertion point by identity walk, not by comparator re-derivation:** walk the
   old witness minus `{left, right}` against the new contact list; the single position where
   they diverge is `p`, and the diverging element is the merged seat. If the walk finds
   anything other than "survivors in preserved relative order plus exactly one insertion"
   (code drift, an unstable-sort regression, a tie behaving unexpectedly), `record_commit`
   **invalidates retention** (sets the option's inner state to poisoned/None) instead of
   guessing — the next round pays one full rebuild and the census counter says so.
2. **Builds the merged bucket** as the linear ascending merge of buckets `left` and `right`
   read from the retained arena — O(|l| + |r|), ~5–10 k entries, microseconds. Deliberately
   recomputed here rather than captured from the accepted proposal's `merged_rows` inside the
   G3 variant loop: it decouples retention from G3's fold internals for the cost of one small
   merge, and gives the equivalence tests a single seam.
3. **Applies the remap:** rebuild `buckets` (1,365 `(u32,u32)` entries — remove two, insert
   one at `p`, order preserved), append the merged payload to the arena, update `cells`
   (`− len(l) − len(r) + len(merged)`), rebuild `contact_witness` by the same
   remove-two-insert-one (merged entry's owner is predicted
   `Specialized(first_specialized_owner + p)`… no: owner ids are positional over the WHOLE
   list, so the witness stores owners recomputed positionally — an O(contacts) pass; survivors'
   generic-vs-specialized resolution is invariant, only the specialized id value shifts with
   position, and the id is `first_specialized_owner + index` by construction).
4. **Compacts the arena** when `arena.len() > 2 × cells`: one O(cells) copy in bucket order,
   amortized over O(cells / avg-merged-bucket) ≈ hundreds of rounds. Worst-case held bytes are
   therefore bounded by `2 × cells × 4 B + (contacts+1) × 8 B` — the charged ceiling (D-G6-4).

Update cost per round: O(|l| + |r| + contacts) ≈ tens of microseconds, plus the amortized
compaction. **This is O(affected) content work; the O(contacts) bookkeeping is 1,365 entries
and is noise.** (A memcpy-splice CSR — no arena, O(cells) memmove ≈ 2–4 ms/round — is an
acceptable fallback if the arena proves fiddly; both preserve visit order and therefore
bit-identity. The arena is recommended; the fallback is recorded so a mid-implementation pivot
is a recorded choice, not a redesign.)

## D-G6-3 — GUARDS AND THE CORRECTNESS STRATEGY

Retention across rounds is a new staleness class — the G2 verification history
(`perception.rs` arm 3: a stale structure meeting a *larger* mesh produced a real
`index out of bounds: the len is 60 but the index is 60` panic) is the standing precedent that
these guards must be mutation-proven, not argued. Four guards, checked in this order at every
`build_gate_load_state` entry, **any miss → counted full rebuild** (rebuild is always correct;
guards exist so it is also rare):

| # | guard | catches | test (each mutation-proven: delete the guard, the named test must fail) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `retained.mesh_fingerprint == verified_mesh.fingerprint()` | a retained transpose meeting a different mesh — retained row indices would index a different eligibility (the perception.rs OOB class) | `fu8f_retained_transpose_refuses_a_foreign_mesh`: build retention under `make_overhang_table`, present `make_toed_slab`'s proof (the G2 arm-3 larger-mesh shape); assert full rebuild taken (counter) and output equals fresh build. Mutation: delete the compare → the retained path runs and the equality assert (or an OOB panic) fails the test. |
| 2 | `retained.contact_witness` equals the fresh `physical` list — length, and per contact `(owner, point_mm)` on `to_bits` (contact_id is positional, implied) | every contact mutation not routed through `record_commit`: `coalesce_exact_regular_seats` before the terminal audit (F-G6-4), attach paths, future drift | `fu8f_retained_transpose_rebuilds_when_contacts_moved_behind_it`: perturb one contact's position between rounds without `record_commit`; assert rebuild counter incremented and output equals fresh build. Also assert the **terminal-audit** call after a fixture coalesce takes the rebuild path. Mutation: delete the compare → equality-vs-fresh assert fails. |
| 3 | the committed merge resolved specialized: `record_commit` re-runs the `merged_seat_is_specialized` predicate on the committed contact (its `generic_owner_for_parts` is `None`) | the one case where merged bucket ≠ union of parents' buckets (F-G6-1) — silent wrong eligibility | `fu8f_retained_transpose_invalidates_on_a_generic_resolving_merge`: fixture whose merged parts resolve to a generic island owner; assert retention invalidated and next round full-builds. Mutation: delete the predicate → the retained-vs-rebuilt harness (below) fails on that fixture. |
| 4 | `contact_rows[c] == retained bucket len` for all `c` (F-G6-5, zero added derivation) | bucket-cardinality drift from any update bug the other guards cannot see | covered by the mutation triad below — each corruption changes some bucket's cardinality or content, and this guard (cardinality) or the harness (content) must catch it; the test asserts WHICH one fired. |

**The equivalence proof (the retained `#[cfg(test)]` oracle exists — use it):**

- `fu8f_retained_transpose_matches_a_fresh_rebuild_after_n_commits` — the bit-identity
  harness. A fixture tournament (extend `fu8d_delta_fixture` and the F3 load fixture; **do not
  duplicate them** — they already carry the tie/steal/no-survivor/displaced-holder cases and a
  known gather order) driven for N ≥ 3 committed merges with retention live. After **every**
  commit, run the full fresh build (production path AND `reference_contacts_x_rows_scan`,
  `perception.rs:2741`) and compare on `to_bits`, field by field: transpose buckets
  (payload and order), `nearest` (contact index and squared distance bits), **all three**
  witness slots (contact and distance), partition rows (`face_id`, `contact_id`, `owner`,
  `carried_area_mm2` bits), `contacts_fingerprint`, `winner`/`won_starts`/`won_rows`,
  `carried_area_mm2` per contact, `violation_count`.
- **Post-sort renumbering arm** (the parent design's riskiest-claim warning): extend
  `fu8d_load_delta_matches_the_per_variant_finalize`'s hand-renumbered fixture with an arm
  where the merged seat's sorted position is **strictly interior** (not first, not last), and
  assert `record_commit`'s identity-walk-derived `p` and remapped witness equal the actual
  `append_variant_load_inputs` output. The FU-8d fixtures pin the *variant* renumbering
  (merged-last); this arm pins the *committed* renumbering (merged-at-sorted-position). They
  are different claims and both stay pinned.
- **The mutation triad (non-vacuity, the FU-8e/G1 way — measured, not argued).** Three
  deliberate corruptions of the update, each applied via a test-only hook, each REQUIRED to be
  caught, with the transcript in the test doc comment: (a) insert the merged bucket at `p + 1`;
  (b) leave `right`'s bucket in place (skip one removal); (c) drop the last row from the merged
  bucket. Each must fail the harness (or fire guard 4) — a harness that stays green under any
  of the three is vacuous and must not land.
- **Live corpus witness:** `make_raking_blade` — 21 committed merges over 23 gate batches, all
  on the retained path, end-to-end digest `13278215294653054002` byte-identical. Plus the full
  OD-8 census: seven frozen digests, `Sphere`/`Cylinder`/`make_box` covering the untouched
  accepted-load path.
- **Env-gated audit mode** (`PLINTH_VERIFY_LOAD_RETENTION=1`, `#[cfg(test)]`-independent,
  census-feature-gated like the scratch probes): every retained round also full-rebuilds and
  compares. Never on by default; it exists so G6d's re-measure can run one belt-and-braces
  treant round-trip if the owner asks.

## D-G6-4 — CHARGING HONESTY

**Standing ruling, restated first: NO resource cap is raised, lowered, or touched.** The
gate/load caps stay where FU-8b's history fixed them; retention removes work and adds one held
byte term, and a falling demand can create no refusal.

- **First-round (and every guard-miss round): charged as a build.** The full path's charge is
  exactly what G5 lands — grouping linearithmic + extension + transpose count/finish + scan.
  Nothing new.
- **Retained rounds: the per-round charged operation model becomes the work the round
  executes:** the G5-repriced scan term (`Σ|eligible_owners| × k` ≈ 10⁸-class) + the update
  term O(|l| + |r| + contacts) + the O(rows) fill + O(contacts) hash + the standing
  `append`/`contact_rows` descriptor charges. The build terms drop off retained rounds' stated
  demand. As with G3, the plan states what the round MAY spend before it spends it: a round
  cannot know at plan time whether a guard will miss, so the honest shape is the G3/FU-8d
  shape — **charge the round for the full build (the admissible worst case), meter the
  executed retained-path spend beside it** (`executed_load_build: Retained | Rebuilt` +
  operation meter), assert executed ≤ charged. This is deliberate: charging the retained cost
  and then guard-missing would execute unafforded work — the exact hole FU-8d part (3) closed.
- **Bytes:** the retained arena's ceiling `2 × cells × 4 B + (contacts + 1) × 8 B` (~66 MB
  treant) + row invariants (~4.7 MB: face_id 4 B + area 8 B + centroid 24 B × 117,741) +
  witness (~54 KB) joins `gate_load_state_bytes` (`specialized.rs:4319`) — the gate-peak term
  that already holds `GateLoadState` across the batch; retention extends lifetime across
  rounds, replacing G1's per-round 32,848,040 B scratch term on retained rounds. ~66 MB is
  12.6 % of the untouched 512 MiB `max_load_scratch_bytes`, noise against the 2.65 GB measured
  stage peak and irrelevant to the ~100 GB machine ceiling — and it is charged anyway.
- **Record sites whose numbers move (the every-record-site rule applies at implementation, and
  G6c MUST rebase on G5's landed figures, which this design cannot know):** the G5-repriced
  scan/demand figures wherever G5 pins them; the 32,848,040 B G1 term everywhere it is quoted
  (`placeholders.rs` `SUPPORT_SPECIALIZED_MAX_LOAD_SCRATCH_BYTES` registry note, `constants.md`
  regenerated by its producer, `support-resource-caps-2026-08-05.md` (new dated section — §13
  is G3's; expect §14 = G5, §15 = G6), `03-physics-and-constants.md` §3.10.8,
  CURRENT-STATUS.md FU-8f ledger row + new G6 section appended never rewritten); doc comments
  at `LoadEligibilityTranspose`, `LoadTransposeCounts`, `load_resource_plan`, `GateLoadState`,
  `gate_load_state_bytes`, `SpecializedLoadBaseline`; `m0-constants-source-ledger.json` if any
  registry note moves. The old numbers 5,543,831,115 / 6,592,813,742 / 9,273,857,317 /
  10,457,910,996 are **G5's sweep, not G6's** — G6c must not re-sweep them, only whatever G5
  replaced them with.

## D-G6-5 — PREDICTED ARITHMETIC, AND WHY D4 MISSED

**Why D4's 0.393 s became the measured 0.93 s — diagnosed from the probe numbers, in one
paragraph.** D4 priced the post-D2a transpose build at "~15 ms riding the existing
`contact_rows` walk", with a caveat of "+315 ms if built naively as the replica did". G1
deliberately landed the self-contained build (its record says so, and why), and the caveat's
own figure was wrong by ~2.6×: the replica's 329.6 ms did **one** per-cell binary-search
derivation pass, while the landed `LoadTransposeCounts` does the `partition_point`-per-
`(row, owner)` walk **twice** — `count` (405 ms) then `finish` (408 ms) — because charging
before allocating requires counting before filling. Add the retained-not-landed D2b column
(grouped 134 + extend 17 stayed) and the under-isolated residuals D4 itself flagged (gather
≤40 ms, probe overhead), and 0.393 + ~0.63 (build) + ~0.12 (grouped/extend, probe-off) ≈ 0.93
reconciles to the measurement. The miss was not the round model — it was pricing a coupling
(D2b's) that the phase plan had deliberately deferred, plus halving instead of doubling the
naive-build cost. G6's prediction below inherits that lesson: it prices only what G6 itself
removes, from the probe's own line items.

**Predicted round (steady state, probe-off, from the G4 numbers).** Probe-off
`build_gate_load_state` ≈ 800 ms mean; scaling the probe-on internal shares (×800/1040 ≈ 0.77):
transpose count+finish ≈ 625, grouped ≈ 103, extend ≈ 13 — **removed ≈ 741 ms**; retained-path
additions (record_commit merge, guards, amortized compaction) ≈ +1–3 ms.

## OPEN QUESTIONS, WITH RECOMMENDED ANSWERS

1. **Arena-indirection or memcpy-splice CSR?** → **Arena**, with the 2× compaction ceiling; the
   splice (O(cells) memmove ≈ 2–4 ms/round, flat memory) is the recorded fallback. Both are
   bit-identical; the arena is O(affected) and the ceiling is charged either way.
2. **Take the merged bucket from the G3 variant loop's `merged_rows` instead of recomputing?**
   → **No.** Recompute in `record_commit` from the retained parents (microseconds). Coupling
   retention to the acceptance fold's internals saves nothing measurable and welds two phases'
   correctness arguments together.
3. **Retire the per-round `owner_faces`/`pair_starts` rebuild (12 ms) by deriving
   `merged_rows` from retained buckets?** → **Defer, measured-and-declined disposition**
   (ruling R-c shape): 12 ms of a ~190 ms round, and `proposal_load_bound` reads
   `contact_rows` from the same walk. Re-measure in G6d before touching it.
4. **Should the terminal audit try to ride retention through `coalesce_exact_regular_seats`?**
   → **No.** One full rebuild per tournament is ~1.7 s of a multi-minute stage; teaching
   `record_commit` about coalescing multiplies the remap proof surface for nothing. Guard 2
   handles it by design.
5. **The 3.32 GiB peak RSS at the G3 tip** (vs 2.446 GiB at FU-8e, unexplained, no counters)
   → G6d must attribute it before the record quotes any G6 memory claim; G6's own +66 MB held
   cannot be allowed to blur into an unexplained +0.9 GB. A finding, not a limit to raise.
6. **Does G5's repricing change this design?** → It changes only G6c's numbers (which must be
   read from G5's landed record, not from here). The retained-round model in D-G6-4 is stated
   relative to "the G5-repriced scan term" precisely so G6 does not fork G5's arithmetic.
7. **`proposal.position_mm` vs the committed seat's position** (parent design open question 6)
   → moot under this design: `record_commit` reads the committed contact's actual fields from
   `contacts_after_sort` via the identity walk, and guard 2 compares against the actual
   `physical` list every round. Nothing here trusts the proposal's copy. The parent's
   suggested `debug_assert_eq!` at `commit_merge` is still worth carrying in G6a as a one-line
   rider.
